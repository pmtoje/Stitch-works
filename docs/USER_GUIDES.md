# User guides

## Customers — booking workspace and machines
1. Go to **Book a Workspace**. Choose the facility and date.
2. Choose a duration: by the hour, half day (morning/afternoon), full day, or multiple consecutive days.
3. Tick every workstation and machine you need — e.g. two straight-stitch machines and an overlocker. Greyed-out items are already booked, held, or under maintenance.
4. Check the price summary (member discounts apply automatically) and press **Reserve & continue to payment**. You’ll be asked to sign in or create an account if needed.
5. Pay securely within the hold time shown (card, transfer or USSD via Paystack). Your booking is confirmed when payment is verified and you’ll receive an email.
6. Manage bookings in **My dashboard → Workspace bookings**: pay, reschedule (same length, before the cancellation window), cancel, and download invoices/receipts.

## Corporate clients — bulk production
1. **Bulk Production → Request a production quote.** Enter your organisation, garments and quantities, sizes, fabric, branding/embroidery/printing, deadline and delivery location. Attach designs, tech packs, measurement charts or logos (up to 10 files).
2. No account? One is created from your email — use the link we email you to set a password.
3. Review the quotation in **My dashboard → Production orders**, accept or request changes.
4. Pay the deposit online. Follow every stage — materials sourcing, cutting, production, quality control, finishing, dispatch — in the order tracker. Pay the balance on dispatch.

## Tailors
1. **Become a StitchWorks Tailor → Apply.** Upload photos/videos of your work and choose what you’re interested in (workspace membership, machine rental, piece-rate contracts, employment, instructing).
2. Track your application in **My dashboard → My tailor application**; you’ll be invited to a practical assessment.
3. Once approved you receive a Tailor ID and profile. Choose a membership plan (**Membership**) for member rates and production-job eligibility.
4. **Production jobs**: see your allocations, submit completed pieces for QC, and apply for open jobs in the marketplace.
5. **Piece-rate earnings**: accepted pieces × rate, approved by the production manager and paid by finance — every payout shows its reference.

## Academy students
1. **StitchWorks Academy** → choose a programme → pick a session → **Apply** (a few minutes).
2. Pay the application fee if shown. You’ll be notified when accepted; pay tuition to secure your place (places are confirmed in order of payment).
3. The **Student portal** shows your schedule, attendance, learning resources, assignments (submit text or files), results and certificate.
4. Your certificate has a unique verification ID that employers can check at **/verify**.

## Account security (everyone)
**My dashboard → Profile & security**: change your password, turn on two-factor authentication, update your phone for SMS/WhatsApp reminders.
